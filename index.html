How to play

  1. Everyone opens this page on their own phone. One person taps Host a game, the others tap Join.
  2. On your turn, your phone shows words from the category of the space you're standing on.
  3. Describe them without saying the word, any part of it, or "rhymes with". The other players shout guesses.
  4. Tap the name of whoever guessed right. You and the guesser each move one space, so it pays to listen.
  5. When time's up, check the list. First player round the wheel and back to the start wins.
  1. Each team picks a describer for the turn. Everyone else on the team guesses.
  2. The describer gets words from the category of the space their team is standing on.
  3. Describe without saying the word, any part of it, or "rhymes with". No acting it out.
  4. Tap Got it when your team shouts the answer, or Pass to skip.
  5. When the timer runs out, check the list and move one space for every word guessed.
  6. First team round the wheel and back to the start wins.

Wildcard spaces mix words from every category, plus some odd extras.
